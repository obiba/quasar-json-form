# Builder playground

<p class="doc-lead">Design a form with <code>QJsonFormBuilder</code>: the outline and its palette, the properties of the selected element, the preview with its language switch, the translations and the source. Import your own form or an angular-schema-form pair, and export the result.</p>

The palette also holds the color control of the [custom controls](#/start/custom-controls) page,
passed through the `catalog` and `renderers` props. The form emitted by `v-model` is shown below the builder.
